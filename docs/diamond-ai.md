# Diamond AI

A research and development project of ArtaBlockchain Team in the field of
**structured training and assessment of AI agents**.

> Diamond AI is in development. This document describes the research program,
> not completed results.

## Research Focus

Methods and infrastructure for structured preparation and evaluation of AI agents:

- curriculum-style training programs for agent roles;
- examination infrastructure with reproducible criteria;
- datasets for training scenarios and evaluation;
- agent memory models for longitudinal assessment;
- testing and reporting infrastructure for experiment tracking.

## Components

| Component | Status | Description |
|---|---|---|
| AI Academy | In development | Structured program for agent preparation |
| Academy Charter | Drafted | Goals, principles and rules of the academy |
| Foundation Module | In development | Base curriculum for agent roles |
| Exam Infrastructure | In development | Examination and scoring framework |
| Datasets | In development | Scenario and evaluation datasets |
| Memory | In development | Agent memory for long-term tracking |
| Testing Infrastructure | In development | Test execution and result collection |
| Reporting Infrastructure | In development | Experiment reporting and audit trail |

## What We Do Not Claim

- We do **not** claim training of neural-network weights.
- We do **not** claim that the academy is fully operational.
- We do **not** claim autonomous task-completion results.

At the time of the last inspection, training outcome reports had not yet been produced;
verified report catalogs were empty. Results will be published only after verification.

## Relationship to Arta AI Team

Diamond AI research informs the role-based training used in
[Arta Business Lab](business-lab.md), and shares evaluation methodology with the
Critic and Learning agents of Arta AI Team.
