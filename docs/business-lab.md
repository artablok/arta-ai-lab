# Arta Business Lab

A multi-agent business laboratory operated by ArtaBlockchain Team. It applies the
Arta AI Team agent framework to business research, analysis and process automation.

> This document describes the lab's public structure only. Internal project names,
> data and operational details are not published.

## Structure

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| Core Office | Central coordination of lab activities |
| Business Office | Business operations and administration |
| Role-based AI training | Training programs tailored to agent roles |
| Knowledge Base | Curated organizational knowledge |
| Agent Memory | Persistent context and experience of agents |
| Business Research | Market/domain research workflows |
| Experiments | Controlled trials and hypothesis testing |
| Metrics | Performance and outcome measurement |
| Project Management | Planning and tracking of initiatives |

## Role Training Programs

Individual base training programs have been prepared for key roles:

- Director
- Strategist
- Analytics
- Critic
- Learning

Programs combine role documentation, supervised practice and evaluation by the Critic
and Director agents.

## Applied Directions

- **Real estate** — research and analytics workflows.
- **Business analytics** — data-driven assessment and reporting.
- **Workflow automation** — repeatable multi-agent pipelines for business processes.

## Status

Internal research and development. The lab's outputs feed the team's projects and are not
publicly released as a product at this stage.

## Confidentiality

Internal project spaces and their contents are excluded from public documentation.
