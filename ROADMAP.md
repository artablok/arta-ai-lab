# Roadmap

Provisional development directions for Arta AI Lab. Timelines are intentionally not
committed here; items are re-prioritized by the team.

## Ongoing

- **Operational hardening of Arta AI Team** — stability of the containerized deployment,
  observability of agent workflows, supervision tooling.
- **Arta Business Lab development** — knowledge base growth, agent memory improvements,
  metrics and experiment frameworks for business research.
- **Diamond AI research** — structured curriculum, examination and evaluation
  infrastructure for AI agents (see [docs/diamond-ai.md](docs/diamond-ai.md)).

## Next Directions

- **Workflow automation** — reusable multi-agent pipelines for repeatable business
  and research processes.
- **Business analytics tooling** — deeper analytical capabilities of the Analytics and
  Research agents, standardized reporting.
- **Domain applications** — application of the multi-agent approach to real estate
  research and related domains, in line with Arta Business Lab.
- **Evaluation methodology** — published, reproducible evaluation criteria for agent
  quality (in the Diamond AI line).

## Non-Goals (at This Stage)

- Autonomous unsupervised operation.
- Public agent-as-a-service offerings.
- Claims of model training results without verified evaluation.

## Reporting

Progress updates are published by ArtaBlockchain Team as they are verified.
This roadmap is a living document and will be revised as results are confirmed.
