# Agent Specializations

Arta AI Team consists of nine specialized agent services. The table below describes
their intended roles; verified runtime behavior is under evaluation and is not claimed
in this document.

> Public role descriptions only. Internal instructions, prompts and operational
> procedures are not published.

## Director
- **Purpose**: team coordination and execution management.
- **Responsibilities**: decomposes goals into tasks, assigns work to specialist agents,
  sequences execution, consolidates outputs, escalates to humans when needed.

## Strategist
- **Purpose**: long-term and strategic thinking.
- **Responsibilities**: scenario analysis, opportunity assessment, strategic option
  framing, alignment of tactical work with higher-level goals.

## Analytics
- **Purpose**: quantitative and qualitative analysis.
- **Responsibilities**: data processing, metric computation, trend identification,
  analytical reporting for decision support.

## Research
- **Purpose**: knowledge acquisition.
- **Responsibilities**: information gathering, source discovery and evaluation,
  structured summaries, fact-checking support.

## Learning
- **Purpose**: capability development of the team and its members.
- **Responsibilities**: training program design, knowledge-base curation, skill
  gap analysis, onboarding of new roles.

## Critic
- **Purpose**: quality assurance and challenge.
- **Responsibilities**: reviews outputs of other agents, identifies weaknesses,
  inconsistencies and risks; provides structured critique before delivery.

## Content
- **Purpose**: content production.
- **Responsibilities**: drafting and editing of texts, documentation, announcements
  and educational materials.

## Community
- **Purpose**: community operations.
- **Responsibilities**: community support flows, engagement, feedback collection
  and summarization for the team.

## Outreach
- **Purpose**: external communications.
- **Responsibilities**: partnership correspondence, public inquiries, coordination
  of external presentations of the team's work.

## Operating Model

1. Humans set goals and constraints.
2. The Director decomposes goals and coordinates the team.
3. Specialist agents execute within their roles.
4. The Critic reviews consolidated outputs.
5. Humans approve final results.

This model keeps decision authority with the team while using agents for execution
speed and breadth.
